# Create Your IBMid for TechZone Access

This guide is for **Business Partners and clients** participating in instructor-led workshops using IBM TechZone environments.

## Who Needs This?

- ✅ **Business Partners** without an existing IBMid
- ✅ **Clients** invited to workshop environments
- ❌ **IBM Employees** - You already have an IBMid, skip this guide

## Important Notes

⚠️ **You MUST create your IBMid BEFORE the instructor invites you to the workshop environment.**

⚠️ **You will NOT receive the workshop invitation email unless you already have an IBMid.**

⚠️ **Your email address becomes your IBMid** - use it to access the workshop environment.

---

## Registration Steps

### Step 1: Access the IBMid Registration Page

Go to: **[https://www.ibm.com/account/reg/signup?formid=urx-19776](https://www.ibm.com/account/reg/signup?formid=urx-19776)**

### Step 2: Enter Your Information

Fill in the required fields:

- **Email address** (this becomes your IBMid)
- **First name**
- **Last name**
- **Company name**
- **Country/Region**
- **Password** (must meet security requirements)

![Create IBMid Registration Form](images/create-your-IBMid.png)

> **Important:** Your email address becomes your IBMid, which you will use to access the workshop environment!

### Step 3: Submit Registration

Click the **Next** button.

### Step 4: Verify Your Email

1. Check your email inbox for a message from IBM
2. The email contains a **one-time verification code**
3. Copy the verification code

### Step 5: Complete Verification

1. Return to the registration page
2. In the **Verification** field, enter the code from your email
3. Click **Create account**

### Step 6: Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation email indicating that:
- Your IBMid account creation was successful
- Your account is now activated
- You can now receive workshop invitations

---

## What Happens Next?

### After Creating Your IBMid

1. ✅ **Notify your instructor** that you've created your IBMid
2. ✅ **Wait for the workshop invitation** email from your instructor
3. ✅ **Accept the invitation** to access the TechZone environment
4. ✅ **Follow the workshop instructions** provided by your instructor

### Workshop Environment Access

Once invited to the workshop:

- You'll receive an email with access instructions
- Use your IBMid (email) and password to sign in
- Access the pre-configured watsonx Orchestrate environment
- No additional setup required - the instructor has prepared everything

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Didn't Receive Verification Email

**Solutions:**
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Wait a few minutes - emails can be delayed
3. Verify you entered the correct email address
4. Try the registration process again

### Issue: Verification Code Expired

**Solutions:**
1. Request a new verification code
2. Complete verification within the time limit (usually 15-30 minutes)

### Issue: Can't Create Account

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure your password meets all requirements:
   - At least 8 characters
   - Contains uppercase and lowercase letters
   - Contains at least one number
   - Contains at least one special character
2. Try a different email address if the current one is already registered
3. Contact your workshop instructor for assistance

### Issue: Still Haven't Received Workshop Invitation

**Solutions:**
1. Confirm your IBMid is activated (check confirmation email)
2. Verify you provided the correct email to your instructor
3. Check spam/junk folders
4. Contact your instructor to resend the invitation

## For Workshop Instructors

If you're an instructor setting up a TechZone environment:

1. **Collect IBMids** from all participants before sending invitations
2. **Verify** that participants have completed IBMid registration
3. **Send invitations** through TechZone to the registered IBMids
4. **Provide** workshop-specific access instructions
